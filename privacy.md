# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: September 21, 2026_

This policy covers all iOS games published by Chris Shain, including **Ricochet Run: Brick Bounce**, **Bloom: Steer the Garden**, and **Bumper Crop Farm Stand**.

## Summary

The games themselves do not collect, store, or share personal data. **Ricochet Run** shows
ads served by Google AdMob, and the ad network collects some data described below. Bloom and
Bumper Crop show no ads.

## Details

- **No accounts.** The games do not require or offer sign-in of any kind.
- **No data collection.** The games do not collect personal information, usage analytics, device identifiers, or location.
- **Advertising (Ricochet Run only).** Ricochet Run shows ads through Google AdMob: an optional
  "watch an ad to keep going" ad you choose to view, and an occasional ad between runs. On first
  launch iOS asks whether to allow tracking; if you decline, ads are still shown but are not
  personalized, and the game works the same. Google may collect a device advertising identifier,
  coarse location, ad interaction and diagnostic data under Google's privacy policy
  (https://policies.google.com/privacy). You can reset or limit the advertising identifier in
  iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking / Apple Advertising.
- **No tracking or advertising SDKs in Bloom or Bumper Crop.**
- **No purchases.** The games contain no in-app purchases.
- **Local data only.** Scores, settings, and saved gardens are stored only on your device and are never transmitted. Deleting the app deletes this data.
- **Sharing.** If you tap Share, the game creates a screenshot and hands it to the iOS share sheet. Nothing is sent anywhere unless you choose an app to share it with. Saving a screenshot to your photo library happens only if you choose that option, which is why the game asks for permission to add to your photos.
- **TestFlight.** If you install a beta build through Apple's TestFlight, Apple may collect crash logs and usage data under Apple's own terms. See Apple's TestFlight privacy information.

## Children

The games do not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.
